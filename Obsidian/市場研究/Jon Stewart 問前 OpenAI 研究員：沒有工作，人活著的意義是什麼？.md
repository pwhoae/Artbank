"There won't be jobs in this world. This is the world that is being envisioned by the people building the technology."  
Former OpenAI and Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon, 28, sits down with Jon Stewart on The Daily Show. He says AI companies genuinely envision a future of no jobs and universal high income. Stewart pushes back: even in jobs we don't love, people need to feel relevant — and when people his age retire, they often die because they're no longer connected to purpose. They end on common ground: no matter where we go, it can't be the decision of a handful of people, and the utopian talk may be a cover for naked ambition.
